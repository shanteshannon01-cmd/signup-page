About

Welcome to The Case File Club, a community for curious true crime lovers. Each week, we open a new case file with a timeline, evidence cards and questions to guide discussions. Share your theory, see what others notice and check the solution when you're ready.

Interactive Features:

  • Open a case file: Reveal clues one at a time.
  • Build the timeline:Drag events into the order you think they happened.
  • Cast your theory:Choose a suspect or an explanation, then compare your theory with other
  • Case closed:Reveal the official ending for the case.
